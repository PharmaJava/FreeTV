Stremio Claude Torrents

This is the torrent engine of FreeTV's Stremio Claude for Samsung. It has no screen: it runs as a service of TizenBrew, and FreeTV starts it when you pick a torrent.

Press Back to leave. You can remove this module in TizenBrew's module list.

Code: github.com/PharmaJava/FreeTV (folder tizenbrew-torrent)
